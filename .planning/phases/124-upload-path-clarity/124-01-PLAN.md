|---|---|
| Untrusted tus creation headers to persisted upload session | Length, metadata, concat URLs, and actor context influence signed claims and durable session state. |
| Signed bearer URL to response/persistence | A credential-bearing URL crosses into a response and the redacting session field. |

| T-124-03 | Denial of Service | POST length and concat inputs | high | mitigate | Retain max-size and malformed-length refusal before persistence plus existing bounded list/token processing and focused tests. |
