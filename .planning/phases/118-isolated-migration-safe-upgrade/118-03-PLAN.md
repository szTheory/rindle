Close the operational safety envelope around the populated move: prove all refusal paths occur before mutation, transaction failures roll back, lock contention times out, and the separately guarded reverse direction is usable only while state remains exactly reversible.

Purpose: Ensure a failed or reversed upgrade cannot strand an adopter in a partial Rindle layout.
Output: Reverse public API, shared reverse-state guard, deterministic failure seams, and atomicity/lock/privilege integration proof.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-118-08 | Denial of Service | ALTER lock acquisition | high | mitigate | Require host `SET LOCAL lock_timeout`, translate timeout boundedly, and prove no session leakage or partial mutation. |
| T-118-09 | Tampering | Destination creation / mid-move failure | critical | mitigate | Keep destination provisioning and all fixed ALTERs in the host transaction; inject failures after schema creation and during the move to prove restoration of the public-only starting state. |
