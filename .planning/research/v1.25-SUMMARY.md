# v1.25 Research Summary

**Milestone:** Maintainer Craft & Feedback Velocity  
**Date:** 2026-08-24

## Evidence Baseline

- `mix quality_signals`, Doctor, SAFE-01, dependency hygiene, warnings-as-errors, and compile-cycle
  checks pass before the milestone.
- Recent PR CI: 29 non-cancelled runs, 13.8-minute median, 15.4-minute p95; the required image consumer
  proof costs roughly 8–9 minutes and currently waits for Quality despite sharing no outputs.
- Coverage is 82.13% over 6,268 executable lines. Preserve the signal; do not chase the percentage.
- Curated Credo debt contains 35 findings (13 cyclomatic, 22 nesting); 34 are outside Admin. Full
  strict Credo reports 143 advisories, mostly low-value design preferences.
- Live non-Admin surfaces contain 646 candidate planning-marker lines and 129 planning-ID test names.
  These counts are a review census, not permission for blind replacement.
- `RuntimeChecks.IntegrationChecks` mixes Mux and GCS diagnostics. Runtime-check and
  upload-maintenance test suites mix several independent behavioral contracts.
- PR coverage uses `--slowest 20`; ExUnit implements that through trace mode and `max_cases: 1`,
  serializing otherwise async tests.

## Decisions

- Use no new dependency, quality framework, test partition, or correctness-critical cache.
- Preserve required image-only proof on PRs and the five-profile proof on main/release.
- Separate provider diagnostics and the two mixed test suites; retain ordered migration/upload
  decision tables and cohesive generated-source owners.
- Retain the schema association cycle. Defer the broad facade/worker and provider configuration cycles
  until reader, recompilation, or change-risk evidence justifies a new abstraction.
- Replace historical narration with current rationale, but keep historical planning archives intact
  and do not introduce an AI-text detector.
- Treat the <=8-minute median / <=10-minute p95 target as a hard closure gate with a documented
  external-runner escape hatch.

## Rejected Approaches

- Blanket Credo cleanup, alias churn, line-count splitting, or helper extraction for metric movement.
- Source-string tests for private module/helper layout.
- Coverage-driven test creation or weakened package-consumer assertions.
- Reopening Admin/operator UI or broad dependency/toolchain work.
