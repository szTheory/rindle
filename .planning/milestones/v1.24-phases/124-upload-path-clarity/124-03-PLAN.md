|---|---|
| Validated PATCH request to filesystem/storage adapter | Untrusted body bytes cross into bounded local temporary storage and configured backing storage. |
| Authorized DELETE to backing storage and durable row | A cancellation crosses two ordered mutation systems with different failure contracts. |

| T-124-03 | Denial of Service | PATCH body drain | high | mitigate | Retain fixed read size, dual byte ceilings, per-PATCH temp cleanup, and no whole-body allocation; high failures block execution. |
| T-124-04 | Denial of Service / Information Disclosure | termination and orphan cleanup | high | mitigate | Preserve auth-before-abort, abort-before-row, bounded non-secret retry marker/logging, and reaper coupling under direct tests. |
| T-124-SC | Tampering | package supply chain | low | accept | No dependency installation or lock mutation occurs. |
</threat_model>
